[h1]Miner[/h1]
A new keeper who digs with explosives.

[h2]Ability[/h2]
Press [b]C[/b] (keyboard) or [b]RT[/b] (gamepad) to drop a mine on the tile you are standing in. After its fuse burns out, the mine blasts a cross through the rock: up, down, left and right. A tile the blast cannot break stops that arm of the cross. A blast that reaches another mine sets it off, so you can build chain reactions. You can rebind the key in Options > Key Bindings.

[h2]Upgrades[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Extra Mine / Mine Belt / Mine Bandolier[/b]: more mines on the field at once (1 → 5).
[*][b]Long Fuse Line / Shaped Charge / Tunnel Blaster[/b]: longer blast (2 → 6 tiles each way).
[*][b]Dense Powder / High Explosive / Core Charge[/b]: harder blasts that break harder rock.
[*][b]Quick Fuse / Hair Trigger[/b]: shorter fuse (2.5s → 1.2s).
[*][b]Detonator[/b]: press Drop Mine with no mine left, or while standing on one, to blow up all your mines at once.
[*]Pickaxe, Jetpack and Strong Back upgrades for mining by hand, flying and carrying.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Built for Dome Keeper 5.0.8.
[*]The Miner uses the Engineer's body and music with a warm tint. Custom art may come later.
[*]Mods turn off the Prestige leaderboards.
[/list]
